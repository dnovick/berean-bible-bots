Reflection Questions
  1. Qamets vs. Qamets Hatuf: Both look identical (אָ). What two pieces of information do you need to determine which one you are looking at?
  2. Shewa types: The shewa sign (מְ) can represent three different things. List them and explain how to tell them apart.
  3. Matres lectionis: The letters א ה ו י can function as vowel letters. For each one, list which vowel or vowels it signals when quiescent.
