# Hophal Paradigm Tables — הָקְטַל / הֻקְטַל

*Hophal paradigm root: קטל — strong triconsonantal root (no weak letters)*
*Both vowel patterns are attested OT-wide; see key below each table.*

---

## Perfect (Qatal)

**Diagnostic marker:** הָ or הֻ prefix (qamets hatuf o-class OR qibbuts u-class) + Patach under R2

| Person | Qal (ref.) | **Hophal — o-class** | **Hophal — u-class** | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3ms | קָטַל | **הָקְטַל** | **הֻקְטַל** | o-class predominates in strong root OT forms |
| 3fs | קָטְלָה | **הָקְטְלָה** | **הֻקְטְלָה** | |
| 2ms | קָטַלְתָּ | **הָקְטַלְתָּ** | **הֻקְטַלְתָּ** | OT: הָשְׁלַכְתָּ (Isa 14:19) |
| 2fs | קָטַלְתְּ | **הָקְטַלְתְּ** | **הֻקְטַלְתְּ** | |
| 1cs | קָטַלְתִּי | **הָקְטַלְתִּי** | **הֻקְטַלְתִּי** | OT: הָשְׁלַכְתִּי (Psa 22:10) |
| 3cp | קָטְלוּ | **הָקְטְלוּ** | **הֻקְטְלוּ** | |
| 2mp | קְטַלְתֶּם | **הָקְטַלְתֶּם** | **הֻקְטַלְתֶּם** | |
| 2fp | קְטַלְתֶּן | **הָקְטַלְתֶּן** | **הֻקְטַלְתֶּן** | |
| 1cp | קָטַלְנוּ | **הָקְטַלְנוּ** | **הֻקְטַלְנוּ** | |

> **Key markers — two forms of the Hophal Perfect:**
> - **o-class (הָ):** qamets hatuf under prefix he — sounds like the vowel in "note." This is the predominant pattern for strong roots in the OT: הָשְׁלַכְתָּ (Isa 14:19), הָפְקַד (Lev 6:4), הָכְלַמְנוּ (1Sa 25:15).
> - **u-class (הֻ):** qibbuts under prefix he — sounds like the vowel in "put." Also attested: הֻשְׁלָכָה (Ezk 19:12), הֻכָּה, הֻגַּד.
> - **Note:** The shureq form (הוּ, as in hollow root הוּמַת) is characteristic of biconsonantal/hollow roots — see Ch29. Strong roots use הָ or הֻ.
> - Universal Hophal identifier: u-class OR o-class vowel under the **prefix consonant** in every form.

## Imperative

**Diagnostic marker:** הָ prefix + Patach under R2 (identical in form to Perfect 3ms and Infinitive Construct)

| Person | **Hophal** | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 2ms | **הָקְטַל** | Same form as Perfect 3ms and Inf. Construct |
| 2fs | **הָקְטְלִי** | |
| 2mp | **הָקְטְלוּ** | OT: הָפְנוּ (Jer 49:8 — Hophal Imper. 2mp of פנה) |
| 2fp | **הָקְטַלְנָה** | |

> **The Hophal Imperative exists — but is extremely rare (1–2 tokens in the entire OT).** The form is identical to the Perfect 3ms and the Infinitive Construct; only context distinguishes them. The Hophal Imperative carries the nuance "cause yourself to be [acted upon]" — a passive command. The sole clear OT example is הָפְנוּ (Jer 49:8): "Turn back! / Be turned back!" (Hophal of פנה, 2mp). The rarity reflects the semantic tension between passivity and command, not a grammatical impossibility.

## Participle

**Diagnostic marker:** מֻ prefix (mem + qibbuts) + Qamets under R2

| Form | **Hophal (passive)** | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ms | **מֻקְטָל** | מ prefix + qibbuts under R1 + qamets under R2 |
| fs | **מֻקְטֶלֶת** | |
| mp | **מֻקְטָלִים** | OT: מֻפְקָדִים (2Ki 22:5) — "those appointed" |
| fp | **מֻקְטָלוֹת** | |

> **Key marker — Hophal Participle:** מ prefix + **qibbuts (ֻ) under R1** + qamets (ָ) under R2. Distinguished from Pual Participle (which also has מ prefix + u-vowel) by: Hophal Participle = **passive of Causative** (Hiphil); Pual = **passive of Intensive** (Piel). The Dagesh Forte in R2 marks Pual; Hophal has no Dagesh in R2 (unless from root assimilation).

## Summary: Hiphil vs. Hophal at a Glance

| Feature | Hiphil (active causative) | **Hophal (passive causative)** |
|---|---|---|
| Perfect prefix | הִ (Hireq) | **הָ (o-class) or הֻ (u-class)** |
| Imperfect prefix vowel | יַ / תַּ (Patach) | **יֻ / תֻּ (qibbuts)** |
| Wayyiqtol prefix | וַיַּ | **וַיֻּ** |
| Participle prefix | מַ (Patach) | **מֻ (qibbuts)** |
| Imperative | Yes — הַקְטֵל | **Extremely rare; הָקְטַל (identical to Perfect 3ms)** |
| Meaning | Causes action | **Passive: caused to undergo action** |

**The single most reliable Hophal identifier: u-class vowel (qibbuts ֻ) or o-class (qamets hatuf ָ) under the prefix consonant — every conjugation.**
