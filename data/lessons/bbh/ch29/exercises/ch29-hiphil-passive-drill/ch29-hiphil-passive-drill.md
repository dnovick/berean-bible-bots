# Chapter 29 — Hophal Weak-Root: Hiphil → Hophal Passive Drill

*BBH Chapter 29 · Hophal Weak Verbs*

---

## Instructions

The **Hophal is the passive of the Hiphil** (or Piel/Qal for roots without a productive Hiphil).
Every Hophal form expresses that its subject *undergoes being caused* to do or experience something.

For each weak-root Hophal form below:
1. **Give the active meaning** — what the Hiphil (or Piel/Qal) of this root means.
2. **Translate the Hophal passive form** in context.

This exercise covers **all eight weak-root classes** of Chapter 29.

The answer key is at the bottom.

## Morphology Reference

| Weak Class | Prefix Vowel | Key Diagnostic |
|---|---|---|
| **I-י/ו (Pe-Yod/Vav)** | Shureq (הוּ/יוּ/מוּ) | R1 quiesces into prefix; only R2–R3 visible |
| **Biconsonantal (II-ו/י)** | Shureq (הוּ/יוּ/מוּ) | Only two root consonants; middle vowel contracts |
| **III-ה (Lamed-He)** | Qibbuts (הֻ/יֻ/מֻ) | Perfect 3ms ends ָה; wayyiqtol apocopates |
| **I-נ (Pe-Nun)** | Qibbuts (הֻ/יֻ/מֻ) | Dagesh forte in R2 (נ assimilated) |
| **I-guttural (Pe-Guttural)** | Qibbuts (הֻ/יֻ/מֻ) | Composite shewa under R1 guttural |
| **III-ח/ע (Lamed-Guttural)** | Qibbuts (הֻ/יֻ/מֻ) | Patach furtive before final ח or ע |
| **III-א (Lamed-Aleph)** | Qibbuts (הֻ/יֻ/מֻ) | Patach → Qamets before quiesced silent א |
| **Geminate (II=III)** | Qibbuts (הֻ/יֻ/מֻ) | Dagesh forte in R2 (= R3); compact form |
