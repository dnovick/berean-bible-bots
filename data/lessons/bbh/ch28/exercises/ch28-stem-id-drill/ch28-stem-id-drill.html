BBH Chapter 28 · Qal, Niphal, Hiphil, and Hophal Stems

By Chapter 28 you have learned all four major stems: Qal, Niphal, Hiphil, and Hophal. This drill presents 24 forms drawn from four strong roots: שׁמר (keep/guard), קטל (kill), כתב (write), and פקד (appoint/muster). For each item: (1) select the stem from the dropdown, (2) select the conjugation and PGN, (3) enter the root and gloss. Click ▶ Answer to check your work.

Key identifiers — Qal: base root vowels; no special prefix. Perfect Qamets–Pathach; Imperfect Hireq–Holem; Participle Holem–Tsere (ms). · Niphal: נִ prefix in Perfect; dagesh-forte in R1 in Imperfect/Wayyiqtol; הִ + dagesh in Imperative/Inf. Abs.; נִ + Qamets in Participle. · Hiphil: הִ prefix (Perfect); a-class prefix vowel + chiriq-yod in 2nd position (Imperfect); הַ in Imperative/Inf. Abs. (= same form, context distinguishes); מַ + chiriq-yod in Participle. · Hophal: הָ prefix (Qamets Hatuf, o-class) in Perfect/Inf. Construct/Imperative; יָ prefix (o-class) in Imperfect/Wayyiqtol; מָ prefix in Participle.
