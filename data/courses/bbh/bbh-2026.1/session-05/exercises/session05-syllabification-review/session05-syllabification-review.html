  1. Single-syllable words: Items 1, 2, 4, 5, 10, and 11 are all monosyllabic. How do you determine whether a monosyllable is open or closed?
  2. Seghol pattern: Items 7, 13, and 15 all share the same syllable pattern (O-C with Seghol in both syllables). What is the classic two-syllable noun pattern this represents (BBH calls it the "segholate" pattern)?
