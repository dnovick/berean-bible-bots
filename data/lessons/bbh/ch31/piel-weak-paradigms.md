**Sources:** Attested forms are drawn from the Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible (OSHB). In paradigm study these theoretical forms represent the expected outputs of the documented phonological rules for each weak root class.

## I-guttural (Pe-Guttural) — Model Root חזק {#pe-guttural}

*Note: In the Piel, R1 always carries a full vowel (Hireq in perfect; Patach in imperfect/participle), so gutturals at R1 rarely need composite shewa. The main diagnostic is the guttural letter itself at R1.*

*Attested Piel forms of חזק: חִזְּקוּ (Neh 2:18 — "they strengthened their hands"), לְחַזֵּק (2 Kgs 12:6 — "to repair the temple"), תְּחַזֵּק (Job 4:3 — "you strengthen weak hands"), חַזְּקוּ (Isa 35:3 — "strengthen weak hands"), וְחִזַּקְתִּי (Ezek 30:24 — "I will strengthen").*

> **Key:** I-guttural Piel forms are nearly identical to strong Piel. Because R1 always carries a full vowel (Hireq/Patach), composite shewa is rarely triggered. Compare I-guttural Qal (where R1 shewa is frequent and composite shewa is prominent) to see why the Piel is the "easiest" stem for I-guttural roots.

## Quick Reference: Piel Weak Class Diagnostics

| Class | Perfect 3ms | Imperfect 3ms | Participle ms | Key diagnostic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strong | דִּבֵּר | יְדַבֵּר | מְדַבֵּר | Hireq + Dagesh forte in R2 + Tsere |
| I-guttural | עִזֵּר | יְעַזֵּר | מְעַזֵּר | Guttural letter at R1; forms otherwise match strong |
| III-ח/ע | שִׁלַּח | יְשַׁלַּח | מְשַׁלֵּחַ | Patach before final ח/ע; patach furtive in open stressed syllable |
| III-א | מִלֵּא | יְמַלֵּא | מְמַלֵּא | Tsere + silent final א |
| III-ה | צִוָּה | יְצַוֶּה | מְצַוֶּה | ה mater final; apocopated wayyiqtol (ה drops) |
| I-נ | נִצֵּל | יְנַצֵּל | מְנַצֵּל | נ at R1 preserved; forms parallel strong |
| I-י | יִסֵּר | יְיַסֵּר | מְיַסֵּר | י at R1 retained; double-yod in imperfect (יְיַ) |
| Biconsonantal (Polel) | קוֹמֵם | יְקוֹמֵם | מְקוֹמֵם | Holem-Vav medial; R3 doubled with Dagesh forte |
| Geminate | הִלֵּל | יְהַלֵּל | מְהַלֵּל | R2 = R3; Dagesh does double duty; identical to strong in form |

**Piel Dagesh forte in R2** is present in every class where R2 can accept it. Where R2 is a guttural (as in נחם: R2=ח), compensatory lengthening of the preceding vowel replaces the Dagesh.
