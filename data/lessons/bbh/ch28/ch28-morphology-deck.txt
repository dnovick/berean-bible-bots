What prefix marks Hophal Perfect of a STRONG root — and how many patterns are there?	TWO patterns: (1) הָ — qamets hatuf (o-class) — predominates in OT, e.g., הָשְׁלַכְתָּ (Isa 14:19), הָפְקַד (Lev 6:4); (2) הֻ — qibbuts (u-class) — also attested, e.g., הֻשְׁלָכָה (Ezk 19:12). Shureq (הוּ) marks hollow roots (Ch29), NOT strong roots.	hophal-diagnostic bbh-ch28
Perfect 3ms · Inf.Cs. · Imperative 2ms of Hophal — what do all three have in common?	All three are identical in form: הָשְׁלַךְ. Context (syntactic position) alone distinguishes them. Imperative is the rarest — only 1 clear OT Hophal Imperative exists: הָפְנוּ (Jer 49:8).	hophal-diagnostic bbh-ch28
