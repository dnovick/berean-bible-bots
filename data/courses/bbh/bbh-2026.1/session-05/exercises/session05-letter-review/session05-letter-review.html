Reflection Questions
  1. Gutturals: Name the four guttural letters. What phonological property do they share, and how does it affect dagesh forte?
  2. Sofit forms: Five letters have word-final sofit forms. Name them all. What does the mnemonic כַּמְנַפַּץ remind you of?
  3. Shin vs. Sin: How do you distinguish Shin (שׁ) from Sin (שׂ)? Can you find both in the word יִשְׂרָאֵל?
