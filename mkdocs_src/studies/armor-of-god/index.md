# The Armor of God — Ephesians 6:10–18

Ephesians 6:10–18 is one of Paul's most celebrated passages. This study approaches it in two directions: tracing each piece of armor back through the letter to where Paul first introduced that theme, and examining the spiritual opposition named in verse 12 — the powers and authorities the armor equips believers to stand against.
