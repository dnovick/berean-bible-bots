Reflection Questions
  1. He vs. Ḥet (items 1–2): Both are gutturals. What distinguishes their sounds, and what does "guttural" mean for dagesh forte?
  2. Sofit forms (items 15–19): Five letters have word-final sofit forms. Name all five base letters. Why does the script need separate final forms?
  3. Shin vs. Sin (items 7–8): The dot placement distinguishes these two. Can you find both in the name יִשְׂרָאֵל?
