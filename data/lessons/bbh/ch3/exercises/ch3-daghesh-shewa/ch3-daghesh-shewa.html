Reflection Questions

  1. Words 8 (מַלְכָּה) and 9 (מִשְׁפָּט) both have a Daghesh Lene in a Begadkephat letter that follows a Silent Shewa. Explain why the Shewa is Silent in each case.
  2. Word 4 (הַבְּהֵמָה) has a Daghesh Forte in ב. Word 11 (דָּבָר) has a Daghesh Lene in דּ. Both are Begadkephat letters. What is the rule that distinguishes Forte from Lene in a Begadkephat?
  3. Word 7 (כִּסֵּה) has two Daghesh marks. Name each and explain the function of each.
