# Ch30 "Spot the Piel" Passage Exercise

Students parse 16 numbered verb forms drawn from three OT passages (Gen 1:22, 28; Exo 19:10–14; Num 22:6–8, 17–18), identifying whether each verb is Piel and supplying conjugation, PGN, root, and stem/function. Distractor verbs from Qal, Niphal, and Hophal stems are included throughout, reinforcing the student's ability to distinguish the Piel's dagesh-forte marker from forms learned in earlier chapters. One unnumbered Pual form (מְבֹרָךְ) appears in the text and is explicitly flagged as preview material (Ch32).
