# Exercise: Consonant-Vowel Reading Drill

**Chapter:** BBH Chapter 2 — Hebrew Vowels
**Type:** Sight reading practice

## Description

A reading-aloud exercise consisting of 15 rows × 10 syllables. Each row uses a single consonant throughout, paired with a different vowel in each position. The instructor calls on individual students to read a row aloud. All vowel types from Chapter 2 appear across the fifteen rows, including matres lectionis and Hateph (composite) shewas.

Rows 7 (Alef), 9 (He), and 14 (Ayin) use guttural consonants and therefore include all three Hateph shewas (Hateph Pathach, Hateph Seghol, Hateph Qamets).

## Vowel coverage

All 19 vowel types from BBH Chapter 2 appear at least once across the 15 rows, including Vocal Shewa (row 5, syllable 10).
