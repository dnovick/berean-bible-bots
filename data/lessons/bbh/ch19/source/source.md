# BBH Chapter 19 — Pronominal Suffixes on Verbs

*Source: Pratico & Van Pelt, Basics of Biblical Hebrew Grammar (BBH), Chapter 19*

---

## 19.1 Introduction (§19.1)

In chapter 9, you learned how to identify pronominal suffixes on nouns, prepositions, and the definite direct object marker. In this chapter, you will learn how to identify pronominal suffixes on Qal Perfect, Imperfect, and Imperative verbs. When a pronoun is the direct object of a verb, it is commonly attached to the definite direct object marker אֵת or אֶת־:

יִשְׁמֹר אֹתָ֫נוּ — he will keep us

שָׁפַט אֹתְךָ — he judged you

However, the pronominal suffix may also be added directly to the verb:

יִשְׁמְרֵנוּ — he will keep us (Qal Imperfect 3ms שָׁמַר with 1cp suffix)

שְׁפָֽטְךָ — he judged you (Qal Perfect 3ms שָׁפַט with 2ms suffix)

Both constructions translate the same way.

---

## 19.2 The Pronominal Suffixes (§19.2)

In general, verbs use Type 1 pronominal suffixes with an objective translation value:

| PGN | Type 1 Suffix | Type 1 Alternate | Objective Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **3ms** | וֹ | הוּ | him (it) |
| **3fs** | ָהּ | הָ | her (it) |
| **2ms** | ךָ | | you |
| **2fs** | ךְ | | you |
| **1cs** | ִי | נִי | me |
| **3mp** | הֶם | ם | them |
| **3fp** | הֶן | ן | them |
| **2mp** | כֶם | | you |
| **2fp** | כֶן | | you |
| **1cp** | נוּ | | us |

## 19.3 Pronominal Suffixes on the Perfect (§19.3)

The Qal Perfect 3ms verb קָטַל with the full range of pronominal suffixes:

| PGN | Qal Perfect 3ms + Suffix | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| **3ms** | קְטָלוֹ | he killed him (it) |
| **3fs** | קְטָלָהּ | he killed her (it) |
| **2ms** | קְטָֽלְךָ | he killed you |
| **2fs** | קְטָלֵךְ | he killed you |
| **1cs** | קְטָלַ֫נִי | he killed me |
| **3mp** | קְטָלָם | he killed them |
| **3fp** | קְטָלָן | he killed them |
| **2mp** | קְטָֽלְכֶם | he killed you |
| **2fp** | קְטָֽלְכֶן | he killed you |
| **1cp** | קְטָלָ֫נוּ | he killed us |

**Notes:**

1. The spelling of the Qal Perfect 3ms verbal stem has changed from קָטַל to קְטָל with the addition of pronominal suffixes — propretonic reduction in the first syllable (קְ) and lengthening of the pretonic vowel to Qamets (טָ).
2. In the Perfect conjugation, the alternate Type 1 pronominal suffixes are preferred for the 1cs (קְטָלַ֫נִי), 3mp (קְטָלָם), and 3fp (קְטָלָן). Both forms of the 3ms suffix appear with some frequency: קְטָלוֹ or קְטָלָ֫הוּ.
3. The 2mp, 2fp, and 3fp pronominal suffixes appear infrequently or are not attested in the Perfect.

## 19.4 Spelling of the Perfect Conjugation with Pronominal Suffixes (§19.4)

| | Qal Perfect Without Suffixes | Qal Perfect Before Suffixes |
|---|---|---|
| **3ms** | קָטַל | קְטָל |
| **3fs** | קָֽטְלָה | קְטָלַ֫ת |
| **2ms** | קָטַ֫לְתָּ | קְטַלְתּ֫ |
| **2fs** | קָטַלְתְּ | קְטַלְתִּ֫י |
| **1cs** | קָטַ֫לְתִּי | קְטַלְתִּ֫י |
| **3cp** | קָֽטְלוּ | קְטָל֫וּ |
| **2mp** | קְטַלְתֶּם | — |
| **2fp** | קְטַלְתֶּן | קְטַלְתּ֫ןּ |
| **1cp** | קָטַ֫לְנוּ | קְטַלְנ֫וּ |

**Notes:**

1. In the 3ms, the vowel under ט (קְטָל) is Qamets, not Qamets Hatuf.

2. In the 3fs, the ◌ָה sufformative is replaced by ◌ָת or ַת before a pronominal suffix:

    קָֽטְלָה + הוּ ➣ קְטָלַ֫תְהוּ (she killed him)

    אָֽכְלָה + נִי ➣ אֲכָלַ֫תְנִי (she devoured me)

    מָֽצְאָה + נוּ ➣ מְצָאַ֫תְנוּ (she found us)

3. When suffixes are added to the 2ms form (קָטַ֫לְתָּ), the sufformative is simply תּ before the suffix:

    קָטַ֫לְתָּ + הוּ ➣ קְטַלְתָּ֫הוּ (you killed him)

    יָדַ֫עְתָּ + וֹ ➣ יְדַעְתּוֹ (you knew him)

    עָבַ֫דְתָּ + נִי ➣ עֲבַדְתַּ֫נִי (you served me)

4. The spelling of the 2fs and 1cs forms are identical before pronominal suffixes (קְטַלְתִּ֫י). The 2fs form with a pronominal suffix occurs only sixteen times in the Hebrew Bible, so when you encounter קְטַלְתִּ֫י with a suffix, expect the 1cs form. The Hireq Yod ending is frequently written defectively as Hireq before the pronominal suffix.

    קָטַ֫לְתִּי + ו ➣ קְטַלְתִּיו (I killed him)

    נָתַ֫תִּי + הָ ➣ נְתַתִּ֫יהָ (I gave her)

    עָשִׂ֫יתִי + ם ➣ עֲשִׂיתִם (I made them)

5. The spelling of the 3cp before a pronominal suffix (קְטָל֫וּ) retains the sufformative (וּ), which may be written defectively as Qibbuts before a suffix:

    קָֽטְלוּ + הוּ ➣ קְטָל֫וּהוּ (they killed him)

    סָֽבְבוּ + נִי ➣ סְבָבֻ֫נִי (they surrounded me)

    יָֽדְעוּ + ם ➣ יְדָעוּם (they knew them)

6. The Perfect 1cp form (קָטַ֫לְנוּ) is spelled קָטַלְנ֫וּ before a pronominal suffix, preserving the sufformative (נוּ). It may also be spelled defectively (קְטַלְנֻ֫):

    קָטַ֫לְנוּ + הוּ ➣ קְטַלְנ֫וּהוּ (we killed him)

    דָּרַ֫שְׁנוּ + הוּ ➣ דְרַשְׁנֻ֫הוּ (we sought him)

    יָדַ֫עְנוּ + ם ➣ יְדַעֲנוּם (we knew them)

7. In the Perfect conjugation of III-ה verbs, the 3ms form drops the ה before the pronominal suffix: רָאָה + הוּ ➣ רָאָהוּ (he saw him).

---

## 19.5 Changes in the Spelling of a Suffix on the Perfect (§19.5)

1. Some suffixes are spelled differently depending on whether they are being added to a Perfect form that ends in a consonant (3ms, 3fs, or 2ms) or one that ends in a vowel. The alternate form of the 3fs suffix (הָ) is used if the verbal stem ends in a vowel, as in קְטַלְתִּ֫יהָ (I killed her). The 3ms suffix וֹ is frequently spelled ו after a vowel, as in נְתַתִּיו (I gave him). These changes are minor.

2. The addition of certain pronominal suffixes to verb forms that end in a consonant will frequently require a connecting vowel between the verbal stem and pronominal suffix. For example, שְׁלָחֵךְ (he sent you) requires a connecting vowel (Tsere). A form like קְטַלְתִּ֫יךָ (I killed you) does not require a connecting vowel because the verb form already ends in a vowel. You are not required to learn all possible connecting vowels; simply be aware that they are used when required (§19.12).

## 19.6 Pronominal Suffixes on the Imperfect (§19.6)

Like the Perfect, Type 1 pronominal suffixes are used with the Imperfect. Additionally, three Nun-suffixes occur with some frequency:

| PGN | Type 1 Suffix | Nun Suffix | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **3ms** | הוּ / וֹ | ֶ֫נּוּ | יִלְכְּדֶ֫נּוּ | he will capture him (it) |
| **3fs** | הָ / ָהּ | ֶ֫נָּה | יִלְכְּדֶ֫נָּה | he will capture her (it) |
| **2ms** | ךָ | ֶ֫ךָּ | יִלְכְּדֶ֫ךָּ | he will capture you |

**Notes:**

1. These suffixes are distinguished by the presence of a נ (Nun). In the 2ms form (ֶ֫ךָּ), the נ has been assimilated and appears as Daghesh Forte in the consonant of the pronominal suffix.
2. Despite the variety of forms, the translation value is the same. The 3ms suffixes וֹ, הוּ, and ֶ֫נּוּ all translate as "him (it)."
3. Though infrequent, Nun-suffixes are also attested in the 1cs (ֶ֫נִּי / ַ֫נִּי) and 1cp (ֶ֫נּוּ).

---

## 19.7 Spelling of the Imperfect Conjugation with Pronominal Suffixes (§19.7)

The changes in the Imperfect with pronominal suffixes are minor: (1) the preformative spelling is unchanged in every form; (2) most Imperfect forms that take a pronominal suffix do not have a sufformative.

| | Qal Imperfect Without Suffixes | Qal Imperfect Before Suffixes |
|---|---|---|
| **3ms** | יִקְטֹל | יִקְטְל |
| **3fs** | תִּקְטֹל | תִּקְטְל |
| **2ms** | תִּקְטֹל | תִּקְטְל |
| **2fs** | תִּקְטְלִי | תִּקְטְלִי |
| **1cs** | אֶקְטֹל | אֶקְטְל |
| **3mp** | יִקְטְלוּ | יִקְטְלוּ |
| **3fp** | תִּקְטֹ֫לְנָה | — |
| **2mp** | תִּקְטְלוּ | תִּקְטְלוּ |
| **2fp** | תִּקְטֹ֫לְנָה | — |
| **1cp** | נִקְטֹל | נִקְטְל |

**Notes:**

1. The one basic change in the strong Imperfect before pronominal suffixes is reduction of the stem vowel from Holem (יִקְטֹל) to Shewa (יִקְטְל) by pretonic reduction. (This reduction has already taken place in the 2fs, 3mp, and 2mp forms.)

    יִקְטֹל + הוּ ➣ יִקְטְלֵ֫הוּ (he will kill him)

    יִקְטְלוּ + נוּ ➣ יִקְטְלוּנוּ (they will kill us)

    וַיִּקְבְּרוּ + הוּ ➣ וַיִּקְבְּרֻהוּ (and they buried him)

2. The reduction from Holem to Shewa does not take place with the 2ms suffix (ךָ) — the Holem is shortened to Qamets Hatuf instead:

    יִשְׁמֹר + ךָ ➣ יִשְׁמָרְךָ (he will observe you)

    יִקְטֹל + ךָ ➣ יִקְטָלְךָ (he will kill you)

    If the verb has a Pathach stem vowel, the Pathach lengthens to Qamets before the 2ms suffix:

    יִשְׁלַח + ךָ ➣ יִשְׁלָחֲךָ (he will send you)

    יִשְׁמַע + ךָ ➣ יִשְׁמָעֲךָ (he will hear you)

3. If the Imperfect form ends in a consonant, a connecting vowel may be used between the verb and pronominal suffix (יִקְטְלֵ֫הוּ with Tsere; יִשְׁמְרֶ֫הָ with Seghol). If the Imperfect form ends in a vowel, no connecting vowel is necessary (יִקְטְלוּנוּ, יִשְׁמִרוּם). You are not required to learn all possible connecting vowels (§19.13).

4. In the Imperfect conjugation of III-ה verbs, forms that end in ֶה drop this ending before the pronominal suffix: יִרְאֶה + ֶ֫נָּה ➣ יִרְאֶ֫נָּה (he will see her).

5. The Imperfect forms of נָתַן (to give), לָקַח (to take), and שִׂים (to put, place) occur frequently with pronominal suffixes:

    וַיִּתֵּן + ם ➣ וַיִּתְּנֵם (and he gave them)

    וַיִּקַּח + הָ ➣ וַיִּקָּחֶ֫הָ (and he took her)

    יָשִׂים + ךָ ➣ יְשִׂימְךָ (he will place you)

## 19.8 Pronominal Suffixes on the Imperative (§19.8)

The suffixes used on the Imperative are the same as those used on the Imperfect.

1. The Qal Imperative 2ms form קְטֹל is spelled קָטְל before a pronominal suffix. The vowel beneath ק is Qamets Hatuf.

    שְׁמֹר + ם ➣ שָׁמְרֵם — (You) observe them!

    שְׁפֹט + נִי ➣ שָׁפְטֵנִי — (You) judge me!

2. The Imperative 2fs (קִטְלִי) and 2mp (קִטְלוּ), both ending in a vowel, do not change before a pronominal suffix.

    תִּפְשׂוּ + ם ➣ תִּפְשׂוּם — (You) seize them!

    דִּרְשׁוּ + נִי ➣ דִּרְשׁוּנִי — (You) seek me!

3. Like the Imperfect, Imperatives with a Pathach stem vowel (שְׁמַע) lengthen the Pathach to Qamets before the pronominal suffix.

    שְׁמַע + נִי ➣ שְׁמָעֵנִי — (You) hear me!

    גְּאַל + ָהּ ➣ גְּאָלָהּ — (You) redeem her!

## 19.9 Parsing (§19.9)

When parsing verbs with pronominal suffixes, identify the verb and also the person, gender, and number of the suffix:

| Form | Stem | Conjugation | PGN | Root | Suffix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| נְתַתִּ֫יךָ | Qal | Perfect | 1cs | נָתַן | 2ms suffix |
| יִלְכְּדֶ֫נּוּ | Qal | Imperfect | 3ms | לָכַד | 3ms suffix |

## 19.10 Summary (§19.10)

1. When a pronoun is the direct object of a verb, it may be attached to either the definite direct object marker (נָתַ֫תִּי אֹתְךָ) or directly to the verb (נְתַתִּ֫יךָ).
2. In general, Perfect, Imperfect, and Imperative verbs use Type 1 pronominal suffixes with an objective translation value. With the Imperfect, three additional Nun-suffixes must be memorized: ֶ֫ךָּ (2ms), ֶ֫נּוּ (3ms), and ֶ֫נָּה (3fs). You must be able to recognize and identify the person, gender, and number of all pronominal suffixes.
3. Become familiar with the spelling changes that occur in the verb stem with the addition of pronominal suffixes to the Perfect, Imperfect, and Imperative.
4. The addition of certain pronominal suffixes to verbal forms ending in a consonant will frequently require a connecting vowel. In general, Perfect verbs prefer a-class connecting vowels (Pathach or Qamets), whereas Imperfect and Imperative verbs prefer e-class connecting vowels (Seghol or Tsere).

## 19.14 Something You Should Consider: The Devil Smelled a Rat (§19.14)

> For the devil smelled a rat, and perceived that if the [biblical] languages were revived a hole would be knocked in his kingdom which he could not easily stop up again. Since he found he could not prevent their revival, he now aims to keep them on such slender rations that they will of themselves decline and pass away. They are not a welcome guest in his house, so he plans to offer them such meager entertainment that they will not prolong their stay. Very few of us, my dear sirs, see through this evil design of the devil.
>
> —Martin Luther, 1524
