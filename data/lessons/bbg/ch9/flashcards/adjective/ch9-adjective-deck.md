# Chapter 9 — ἀγαθός Adjective Paradigm Deck

*16 cards covering all distinct inflected forms of ἀγαθός, -ή, -όν (good) across all three genders (2-1-2 pattern), four cases, and both numbers.*
*Import `ch9-adjective-deck.txt` directly into Anki (File → Import).*

---

## How to Use

- **Front of card:** inflected Greek form
- **Back of card:** gender · number · case (all possible parsings for ambiguous forms)
- **Tags** allow filtering by gender, number, or case in Anki

> **Key note on ambiguous forms:**
> Several forms of ἀγαθός are identical across different parsings. The back of each
> card lists ALL valid parsings for that surface form — this is by design. When reading
> Greek text, the article (ὁ, ἡ, τό) or the noun being modified will usually clarify
> which parsing applies; these cards train awareness of the full ambiguity space.

## Ambiguous Forms Summary

| Form | All valid parsings |
|---|---|
| ἀγαθοῦ | M. Gen. Sg · N. Gen. Sg |
| ἀγαθῷ | M. Dat. Sg · N. Dat. Sg |
| ἀγαθόν | M. Acc. Sg · N. Nom. Sg · N. Acc. Sg |
| ἀγαθῶν | M. Gen. Pl · F. Gen. Pl · N. Gen. Pl |
| ἀγαθοῖς | M. Dat. Pl · N. Dat. Pl |
| ἀγαθά | N. Nom. Pl · N. Acc. Pl |

6 of the 16 distinct forms carry 2–3 possible parsings. In context, the definite article
disambiguates: τοῦ ἀγαθοῦ (Gen Sg Masc/Neut), τῆς ἀγαθῆς (Gen Sg Fem), τῶν ἀγαθῶν (Gen Pl, any gender).
