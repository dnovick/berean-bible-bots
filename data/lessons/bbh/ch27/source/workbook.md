### III. Grammatical Commentary

**Genesis 9:8**

**לֵאמֹר** — The Qal Infinitive Construct of אמר with the preposition לְ is an idiomatic formula introducing direct speech. It is essentially untranslatable but may be rendered "saying."

**Genesis 9:9**

**וַאֲנִי הִנְנִי** — The combination of the independent pronoun אֲנִי and the particle הִנֵּה with the 1cs pronominal suffix הִנְנִי is emphatic: "And I, behold, I am…" This construction emphasizes the subject of the following clause.

**מֵקִים** — Hiphil Participle ms from קוּם (Biconsonantal). The Participle with הִנֵּה (or הִנְנִי) indicates an action about to take place or currently in progress: "I am hereby establishing."

**אֶת־בְּרִיתִי** — The direct object marker אֵת followed by the noun בְּרִית with the 1cs pronominal suffix. A covenant (בְּרִית) is a formal, binding agreement between parties.

**Genesis 9:10**

**בָּעוֹף** — The noun עוֹף with the ב preposition and definite article. The ב is instrumental or locative; the phrase "in the birds" refers collectively to all birds.

**יֹצְאֵי** — Qal Participle mp construct of יצא. The form is in construct with הַתֵּבָה. Translate "those going out of the ark."

**Genesis 9:11**

**וַהֲקִמֹתִי** — Hiphil Perfect 1cs from קוּם with Waw Consecutive. The vowel under the ה changes from חִ to הֲ because the following letter (ק) has a reduced vowel. Translate "and I will establish."

**יִכָּרֵת** — Niphal Imperfect 3ms from כרת. Note the gemination of the ר and the Tsere (יִכָּרֵת → Niphal imperfect pattern). Translate "will be cut off."

**מִמֵּי הַמַּבּוּל** — The preposition מִן attached to the construct plural of מַיִם (water): "from the waters of the flood."

**לְשַׁחֵת** — Piel Infinitive Construct from שחת: "to destroy." The ל marks purpose.

**Genesis 9:12**

**זֹאת** — The feminine demonstrative pronoun "this" functions as the predicate of a verbless clause: "This is the sign of the covenant."

**נֹתֵן** — Qal Participle ms from נתן, acting as a present-tense verb: "I am giving."

**לְדֹרֹת עוֹלָם** — "For eternal generations" or "for all generations to come." The noun דּוֹר (generation) appears in the plural construct form.

**Genesis 9:13**

**אֶת קַשְׁתִּי** — The direct object marker with קֶשֶׁת (bow) and the 1cs suffix. Placed first for emphasis: "My bow I have placed…"

**נָתַתִּי** — Qal Perfect 1cs from נתן. The Daghesh Forte in the תּ represents the assimilated נ of the root (I-נ weak verb pattern).

**בֶּעָנָן** — בְּ preposition with the noun עָנָן (cloud) and the definite article: "in the cloud."

**וְהָיְתָה לְאוֹת** — The verb הָיָה with the ל of predicate: "and it will be as/for a sign."
