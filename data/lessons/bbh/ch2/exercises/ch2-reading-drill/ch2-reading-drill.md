# Chapter 2 — Consonant-Vowel Reading Drill

**Topic:** Hebrew Vowels — Sight Reading Practice

---

**Instructions:** Read each line aloud, pronouncing each consonant-vowel syllable in order from left to right. Each line uses one consonant throughout. Your instructor will call on individual students to read a line. All vowel types from Chapter 2 appear across the fifteen lines, including matres lectionis and Hateph shewas.

*Note: Lines 7, 9, and 14 use guttural consonants (א, ה, ע) and therefore include Hateph (composite) shewas. An asterisk (*) marks guttural rows.*
