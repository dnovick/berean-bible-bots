The Hophal is the passive of the Hiphil (or Piel/Qal for roots without a productive Hiphil). For each weak-root Hophal form below:

  1. Give the active meaning — what the Hiphil (or Piel/Qal) of this root means.
  2. Translate the Hophal passive form in context.

Morphological key: I-י/ו & Biconsonantal: הוּ/יוּ/מוּ prefix (Shureq). All other weak classes: הֻ/יֻ/מֻ prefix (Qibbuts). I-נ: Dagesh forte in R2.   I-guttural: Composite Shewa under R1.   III-ח/ע: Pathach furtive before final guttural.   III-א: Qamets before silent final א.   Geminate: Dagesh forte in R2 (= R3).
