Ch29 Hophal Weak Paradigm Drill

Stem: Hophal  |  Roots: ילד · קום · עמד · נגד · גלה · שׁלח · מצא · נקם

For each row, the root and PGN are given. Select the correct Hophal weak form from the dropdown. Options include forms from Qal, Hiphil, Niphal, Piel, and Hophal — and from other Hophal conjugations of the same root. Identify the correct Hophal form for the PGN shown.
Prefix vowel groups: Shureq (הוּ/יוּ/מוּ) — I-י/ו and Biconsonantal roots.   Qibbuts (הֻ/יֻ/מֻ) — I-נ, I-guttural, III-ה, III-ח/ע, III-א, and Geminate roots.
I-guttural: Seghol (הֶ) under prefix in the Perfect before ע or א; Qibbuts (יֻ) in the Imperfect (normal).   III-ה: Perfect 3ms ends ָה; Imperfect 3ms ends ֶה; Wayyiqtol is apocopated (ה drops).   I-נ / Geminate: Dagesh forte in R2.
