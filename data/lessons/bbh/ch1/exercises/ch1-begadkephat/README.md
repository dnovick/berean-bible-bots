# ch1-begadkephat — Hebrew Begadkephat Pronunciation Drill

[← Back to Chapter 1](../../lesson.md)

## Description

A 12-item pronunciation identification drill covering all six begadkephat letters in both their hard (with dagesh lene) and soft (without dagesh) forms. Students see the Hebrew letter and must select the correct pronunciation description from a dropdown of four plausible options.

## Letters Covered

| # | Letter | Name | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | פ | Pe (soft) | f as in fill |
| 2 | גּ | Gimel (hard) | g as in good |
| 3 | תּ | Taw (hard) | t as in tall |
| 4 | ב | Bet (soft) | v as in vine |
| 5 | כּ | Kaf (hard) | k as in king |
| 6 | ד | Dalet (soft) | d as in door |
| 7 | בּ | Bet (hard) | b as in boy |
| 8 | ת | Taw (soft) | t as in tall |
| 9 | כ | Kaf (soft) | kh as in Bach |
| 10 | דּ | Dalet (hard) | d as in door |
| 11 | ג | Gimel (soft) | g as in good |
| 12 | פּ | Pe (hard) | p as in pool |
