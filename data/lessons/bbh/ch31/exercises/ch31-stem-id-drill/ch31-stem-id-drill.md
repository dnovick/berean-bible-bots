# Chapter 31 — Stem Identification Drill (Weak Roots)

*BBH Chapter 31 · Piel Weak Verbs*

---

## Instructions

Below are 25 Hebrew verb forms from weak roots (ברך, גלה, צוה, חזק). For each form, identify:

1. **Root**
2. **Stem** — Qal / Niphal / Hiphil / Hophal / Piel
3. **Conjugation** — Perfect / Imperfect / Wayyiqtol / Imperative / Participle
4. **PGN** — Person-Gender-Number (where applicable)

Leave the answer columns blank, then check your work against the answer key below.

**Key markers for weak roots:**

- **I-Guttural (ברך, חזק):** Piel R2 cannot take dagesh — watch for virtual doubling (longer vowel before R2 instead of dagesh); or in some forms, the guttural itself affects the surrounding vowels.
- **III-ה (גלה, צוה):** Piel perfect 3ms ends in ָּה (Qamets + dagesh-he). Qal perfect 3ms ends in ָה (Qamets-he, no dagesh). Hiphil prefix הִ identifies Hiphil. Niphal shows assimilated נ or נִ prefix. Hophal prefix הֻ (u-class) identifies Hophal passive.
- **Wayyiqtol:** waw + Pathach (וַ) + prefix; the dagesh in the prefix consonant marks wayyiqtol for Qal; Piel wayyiqtol keeps Shewa under prefix.
