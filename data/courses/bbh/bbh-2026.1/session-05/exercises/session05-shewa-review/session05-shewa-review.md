# Session 5 — Shewa: Vocal vs. Silent

**Topic:** BBH Ch3 Review — Identifying Vocal and Silent Shewa

---

**Instructions:** For each word, a specific letter under which a shewa appears is identified in the "Letter" column. Determine whether the shewa is Vocal or Silent, and state the rule that applies.

**Rules to apply:**
- **Vocal Shewa** (מְ): (1) word-initial position, (2) immediately after another shewa, (3) under a letter with Dagesh Forte
- **Silent Shewa** (מְ): (1) word-final position, (2) all other positions (closes a syllable)
- **Hateph Shewa** (מֲ מֱ מֳ): always vocal — composite shewa under gutturals; treated as a short vowel

## Answer Key

| # | Word | Letter with Shewa | Shewa Type | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | בְּרֵאשִׁית | בּ | **Vocal** | Word-initial position — every word begins with a vowel sound; the shewa under the initial Bet is vocal (murmur /ə/) |
| 2 | מֶלֶךְ | ךְ | **Silent** | Word-final position — the shewa under word-final Kaf marks the close of the last syllable; no sound; the word = מֶ-לֶךְ |
| 3 | יְרוּשָׁלַיִם | יּ | **Vocal** | Word-initial position — the Yod begins the word; its shewa is vocal |
| 4 | מִשְׁפָּט | שׁ | **Silent** | Middle-of-word position, not word-initial, not after another shewa, no dagesh forte under שׁ — the shewa closes the first syllable מִשׁ and is silent |
| 5 | בְּרִית | בּ | **Vocal** | Word-initial position — the Bet begins the word; its shewa is vocal |
| 6 | יִשְׂרָאֵל | שׂ | **Silent** | Middle-of-word position after the short Hireq vowel of יִ — the shewa under Sin closes the first syllable יִשׂ and is silent |
| 7 | כְּבוֹד | כּ | **Vocal** | Word-initial position — the Kaf begins the word; its shewa is vocal |
| 8 | נִשְׁמַר | שׁ | **Silent** | Middle-of-word position after the short Hireq of נִ — the shewa under Shin closes the first syllable נִשׁ and is silent |
| 9 | שְׁמַע | שׁ | **Vocal** | Word-initial position — the Shin begins the word; its shewa is vocal |
| 10 | יִשְׁלַח | שׁ | **Silent** | Middle-of-word position after the short Hireq of יִ — the shewa under Shin closes the first syllable יִשׁ and is silent |

---

## Pattern Observations

**Vocal shewa positions in this exercise:** Items 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 — all word-initial.

**Silent shewa positions in this exercise:** Items 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 — either word-final or middle-of-word after a short vowel.

**Note on מִשְׁפָּט (#4):** The silent shewa under שׁ causes the syllable division מִשׁ | פָּט. This is why the פּ receives a Dagesh Forte — it follows a closed syllable.
