# Chapter 34 — Hithpael Strong Morphology Deck

> **Scope note:** This deck provides diagnostic-highlights coverage — not all PGN forms for every conjugation. Weqatal forms are included for 3ms, 2ms, and 1cs. For full paradigm practice across all PGN slots, use the paradigm drill exercise. **Special note:** the Hithpael Perfect 3ms, Imperative 2ms, Infinitive Construct, and Infinitive Absolute are all identical in form (הִתְקַדֵּשׁ) — context alone distinguishes them.

26 cards covering 2 roots across the main Hithpael conjugations, plus metathesis, assimilation, and Hishtaphel identification cards.

**Roots:**
- **קדש** (intensive-reflexive) — to sanctify oneself
- **חזק** (reflexive / simple action) — to strengthen oneself, take courage
- **שׁמר** (metathesis) — to guard oneself → הִשְׁתַּמֵּר
- **סתר** (metathesis) — to hide oneself → הִסְתַּתֵּר
- **דבר** (assimilation) — to speak with → הִדַּבֵּר
- **שׁחה** (Hishtaphel) — to bow down, worship → הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה

| Card Front | Expected Back |

| What are the THREE universal Hithpael diagnostics? | הִתְ prefix (varies by conjugation: יִתְ/תִּתְ Imperfect, מִתְ Participle) + Pathach under R1 + Daghesh Forte in R2 |

| What is הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה, and why is it not Hithpael? | Hishtaphel of שׁחה — the unique Hishtaphel stem (only verb in this stem); 173× in OT = "he worshiped / bowed down" |
