Session 5 — Shewa: Vocal vs. Silent

BBH 2026.1 · Ch3 Review · 10 items

Instructions: For each word, a target letter bearing a shewa is identified in the "Target" column. Determine whether the shewa is Vocal or Silent, and state the rule that applies.
Key Rules:
  • Vocal Shewa: (1) word-initial position, (2) after another shewa, (3) under a letter with Dagesh Forte.
  • Silent Shewa: (1) word-final position, (2) all other positions — closes a syllable.
  • Hateph Shewa (composite: מֲ מֱ מֳ): always vocal — used under gutturals instead of simple vocal shewa.
