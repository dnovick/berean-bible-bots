## Notes on Selected Items

**Word 3 — בַּיִת:** The Yod here functions as a consonant (בַּיִת = "house"), not as a mater lectionis. It receives a Hireq vowel under it, which begins the second syllable. Compare with מִי where the Yod is quiescent (mater for Hireq Yod).

**Word 9 — פֶּה:** The final He has no vowel under it and no mappiq dot. This makes it a quiescent mater lectionis (vowel letter), not a consonant. The result is one open syllable ending in the vowel sound (Seghol He = short E with final He as mater).

**Word 12 — אֱמֶת:** The Alef takes a Hateph Seghol (composite shewa) because Alef is a guttural. The syllable אֱ is still open — a composite shewa under a guttural functions as a reduced vowel, not a syllable-closer.
