## Cross-Corpus Snapshot

| Corpus | Top Name | Count | % of Corpus Names |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hebrew OT | YHWH (יהוה) | 6,513 | 65.8% |
| LXX | Kyrios (κύριος) | 7,667 | 70.3% |
| Greek NT | Theos (θεός) | 1,343 | 30.2% |

**Key observation:** Kyrios in the LXX is the standard rendering of both YHWH and Adonai — its dominance (70.3%) mirrors YHWH's dominance in the Hebrew (65.8%). In the NT, the landscape shifts: Iesous and Christos together account for 35% of divine title occurrences, reflecting the NT's Christological focus.
