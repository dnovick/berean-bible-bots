# BBH Chapter 34 — Hithpael Strong Verbs (הִתְקַטֵּל)

*Basics of Biblical Hebrew, Pratico & Van Pelt, Chapter 34*

---

## 1. Introduction (§34.1)

The Hithpael is the easiest of the derived stems to recognize because of its distinctive preformatives and prefixes. Every Hithpael strong verb form carries three diagnostic features:

1. A distinctive prefix in all conjugations (הִתְ in the Perfect; יִתְ / תִּתְ / אֶתְ / נִתְ in the Imperfect; מִתְ in the Participle)
2. **Pathach under the first consonant of the verbal root**
3. **Daghesh Forte in the second consonant of the verbal root**

The Piel, Pual, and Hithpael all share the Daghesh Forte in R2.

---

## 2. Meaning of the Hithpael (§34.2)

### 1. Intensive-Reflexive

The most common Hithpael function. The subject performs the verbal action upon itself.

| Form | Translation | Analysis |
|---|---|---|
| הִתְקַדֵּשׁ | he sanctified himself | Hithpael Perfect 3ms from קָדַשׁ |
| הִתְחַבְּאוּ | they hid themselves | Hithpael Perfect 3cp from חָבָא |

### 2. Reciprocal

Multiple subjects perform the action on one another.

| Form | Translation | Analysis |
|---|---|---|
| הִתְרָאוּ | they looked at each other | Hithpael Perfect 3cp from רָאָה |
| הִתְקַשְּׁרוּ | they conspired with each other | Hithpael Perfect 3cp from קָשַׁר |

### 3. Iterative

Repeated or habitual action.

| Form | Translation | Analysis |
|---|---|---|
| הִתְהַלֵּךְ | he walked back and forth | Hithpael Perfect 3ms from הָלַךְ |
| הִתְהַפֵּךְ | he turned this way and that | Hithpael Perfect 3ms from הָפַךְ |

### 4. Simple Action

Some Hithpael verbs are translated like a Qal simple action.

| Form | Translation | Analysis |
|---|---|---|
| הִתְפַּלֵּל | he prayed | Hithpael Perfect 3ms from פָּלַל |
| הִתְיַצֵּב | he stood firm | Hithpael Perfect 3ms from יָצַב |

## 3. Hithpael Perfect (§34.3–34.4)

The Hithpael Perfect is formed with the הִתְ prefix, the verbal root (with Daghesh Forte in R2), and the Perfect sufformatives.

| PGN | Qal | Hithpael | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3ms | קָטַל | הִתְקַטֵּל | he slaughtered himself |
| 3fs | קָֽטְלָה | הִתְקַטְּלָה | she slaughtered herself |
| 2ms | קָטַ֫לְתָּ | הִתְקַטַּ֫לְתָּ | you slaughtered yourself |
| 2fs | קָטַלְתְּ | הִתְקַטַּלְתְּ | you slaughtered yourself |
| 1cs | קָטַ֫לְתִּי | הִתְקַטַּ֫לְתִּי | I slaughtered myself |
| 3cp | קָֽטְלוּ | הִתְקַטְּלוּ | they slaughtered themselves |
| 2mp | קְטַלְתֶּם | הִתְקַטַּלְתֶּם | you slaughtered yourselves |
| 2fp | קְטַלְתֶּן | הִתְקַטַּלְתֶּן | you slaughtered yourselves |
| 1cp | קָטַ֫לְנוּ | הִתְקַטַּ֫לְנוּ | we slaughtered ourselves |

**Diagnostics:**
1. הִתְ prefix in every form
2. Pathach under R1
3. Daghesh Forte in R2

## 4. Hithpael Imperfect (§34.5–34.6)

The Hithpael Imperfect uses the preformatives יִתְ / תִּתְ / אֶתְ / נִתְ with the verbal root and Imperfect sufformatives.

| PGN | Qal | Hithpael | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3ms | יִקְטֹל | יִתְקַטֵּל | he will slaughter himself |
| 3fs | תִּקְטֹל | תִּתְקַטֵּל | she will slaughter herself |
| 2ms | תִּקְטֹל | תִּתְקַטֵּל | you will slaughter yourself |
| 2fs | תִּקְטְלִי | תִּתְקַטְּלִי | you will slaughter yourself |
| 1cs | אֶקְטֹל | אֶתְקַטֵּל | I will slaughter myself |
| 3mp | יִקְטְלוּ | יִתְקַטְּלוּ | they will slaughter themselves |
| 3fp | תִּקְטֹ֫לְנָה | תִּתְקַטֵּ֫לְנָה | they will slaughter themselves |
| 2mp | תִּקְטְלוּ | תִּתְקַטְּלוּ | you will slaughter yourselves |
| 2fp | תִּקְטֹ֫לְנָה | תִּתְקַטֵּ֫לְנָה | you will slaughter yourselves |
| 1cp | נִקְטֹל | נִתְקַטֵּל | we will slaughter ourselves |

**Diagnostics:**
1. Preformatives יִתְ / תִּתְ / אֶתְ / נִתְ — unique and easy to identify
2. Pathach under R1
3. Daghesh Forte in R2

## 5. Hithpael Imperative (§34.7)

The Hithpael Imperative is formed with the הִתְ prefix, the verbal root (with Daghesh Forte in R2), and the Imperative sufformatives.

| PGN | Qal | Hithpael | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2ms | קְטֹל | הִתְקַטֵּל | slaughter yourself! |
| 2fs | קִטְלִי | הִתְקַטְּלִי | slaughter yourself! |
| 2mp | קִטְלוּ | הִתְקַטְּלוּ | slaughter yourselves! |
| 2fp | קְטֹ֫לְנָה | הִתְקַטֵּ֫לְנָה | slaughter yourselves! |

---

## 6. Hithpael Infinitive Construct and Infinitive Absolute (§34.8)

Both the Infinitive Construct and Infinitive Absolute are identical to the Perfect 3ms and Imperative 2ms: **הִתְקַטֵּל**. Context distinguishes these four identical-looking forms.

**Shared diagnostics (§34.9):**
1. הִתְ prefix
2. Pathach under R1
3. Daghesh Forte in R2

---

## 7. Hithpael Participle (§34.10–34.11)

The Hithpael Participle uses the מִתְ prefix.

| | Qal | Hithpael | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| ms | קֹטֵל | מִתְקַטֵּל | slaughtering himself |
| fs | קֹטֶ֫לֶת | מִתְקַטֶּ֫לֶת | slaughtering herself |
| mp | קֹטְלִים | מִתְקַטְּלִים | slaughtering themselves |
| fp | קֹטְלוֹת | מִתְקַטְּלוֹת | slaughtering themselves |

**Diagnostics:**
1. מִתְ prefix — unique; makes the Hithpael Participle easy to identify
2. Pathach under R1
3. Daghesh Forte in R2

## 8. Summary Paradigm (§34.14)

In every Hithpael strong verb form: Pathach under R1, Daghesh Forte in R2. The prefix/preformative changes by conjugation.

| PGN | Perfect | Imperfect | Imperative | Inf. Cs. | Inf. Abs. | Participle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3ms | הִתְקַטֵּל | יִתְקַטֵּל | הִתְקַטֵּל | הִתְקַטֵּל | הִתְקַטֵּל | מִתְקַטֵּל |
| 3fs | הִתְקַטְּלָה | תִּתְקַטֵּל | | | | מִתְקַטֶּ֫לֶת |
| 2ms | הִתְקַטַּ֫לְתָּ | תִּתְקַטֵּל | הִתְקַטְּלִי | | | מִתְקַטְּלִים |
| 2fs | הִתְקַטַּלְתְּ | תִּתְקַטְּלִי | הִתְקַטְּלוּ | | | מִתְקַטְּלוֹת |
| 1cs | הִתְקַטַּ֫לְתִּי | אֶתְקַטֵּל | | | | |
| 3cp | הִתְקַטְּלוּ | יִתְקַטְּלוּ | | | | |

| 2fp | הִתְקַטַּלְתֶּן | תִּתְקַטֵּ֫לְנָה | | | | |

## 9. Metathesis (§34.12)

When the ת of the Hithpael prefix or preformative precedes a verbal root beginning with a sibilant consonant (ס, צ, שׂ, or שׁ), the two consonants switch places.

| Qal | | Hithpael | |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁמַר | he guarded | הִשְׁתַּמֵּר | he guarded himself |
| שָׂכַר | he hired | הִשְׂתַּכֵּר | he hired himself (earned wages) |
| סָתַר | he hid | הִסְתַּתֵּר | he hid himself |

> The most important form to know: **הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה** (bow down, worship) from root שׁחה — the most frequent Hithpael form in the OT (~173 occurrences). See §11 for the full Hishtaphel treatment.

---

## 10. Assimilation of ת (§34.13)

When the first consonant of a verbal root is ד, ז, ט, or ת, the ת of the Hithpael prefix assimilates into that consonant and becomes a Daghesh Forte.

| Qal | | Hithpael | |
|---|---|---|---|
| דָּבַר | he spoke | הִדַּבֵּר | he spoke with |
| טָמֵא | he was defiled | הִטַּמֵּא | he defiled himself |

## 11. Additional Information: The Hishtaphel Stem (§34.16)

The verb חָוָה (to bow down, worship) appears **173 times** in the Hebrew Bible and only in the **Hishtaphel** stem — the only verb in this stem. Its forms are recognized by the distinctive prefix הִשְׁתּ (Perfect) and מִשְׁתּ (Participle). Note that חָוָה is a III-ה verb and carries the characteristic endings of that weak class.

| Conjugation | Prefix | Form | PGN | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Perfect | הִשְׁתּ | הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה | 3ms | he worshiped |
| | | הִשְׁתַּחֲווּ | 3cp | they worshiped |
| | | הִשְׁתַּחֲוִיתֶם | 2mp | you worshiped |
| Imperfect | יִשְׁתּ | יִשְׁתַּחֲוֶה | 3ms | he will worship |
| | | וַיִּשְׁתַּחוּ | 3ms | he worshiped (Waw Consecutive) |
| | | תִּשְׁתַּחֲוֶה | 2ms | you will worship |
| | | אֶשְׁתַּחֲוֶה | 1cs | I will worship |
| | | יִשְׁתַּחֲווּ | 3mp | they will worship |
| Imperative | הִשְׁתּ | הִשְׁתַּחֲווּ | 2mp | worship! |
| Inf. Construct | הִשְׁתּ | הִשְׁתַּחֲוֹת | | to worship, bow down |
| Participle | מִשְׁתּ | מִשְׁתַּחֲוֶה | ms | worshiping, bowing down |
| | | מִשְׁתַּחֲוִים | mp | worshiping, bowing down |

**Biblical examples:**

> וְהִשְׁתַּחֲווּ לַיהוָה בְּהַר הַקֹּדֶשׁ
>
> And they will worship Yahweh on the holy mountain. (Isa 27:13)

> וַיִּשְׁתַּחוּ אַבְרָהָם לִפְנֵי עַם הָאָרֶץ
>
> And Abraham bowed down before the people of the land. (Gen 23:12)

> נָפְלוּ לִפְנֵי יְהוָה לְהִשְׁתַּחֲוֹת לַיהוָה
>
> They fell down before Yahweh to worship Yahweh. (2 Chr 20:18)
