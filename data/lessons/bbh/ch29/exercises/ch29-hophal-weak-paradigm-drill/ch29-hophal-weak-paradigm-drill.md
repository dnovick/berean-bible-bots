# Ch29 Hophal Weak Paradigm Drill

**Stem:** Hophal | **Roots:** ילד · קום · עמד · נגד · גלה · שׁלח · מצא · נקם

**Instructions:** For each row, the root and PGN are given. Select (HTML) or write (print) the correct Hophal weak form. Options include Qal, Hiphil, Niphal, and Hophal forms — choose the correct Hophal form for the PGN shown.

**Prefix vowel key:** Shureq (הוּ/יוּ/מוּ) — I-י/ו and Biconsonantal roots. Qibbuts (הֻ/יֻ/מֻ) — all other classes. I-guttural: Seghol (הֶ) in the Perfect prefix before ע or א. III-ה: ָה in Perfect 3ms, ֶה in Imperfect; Wayyiqtol is apocopated. I-נ/Geminate: Dagesh forte in R2.
