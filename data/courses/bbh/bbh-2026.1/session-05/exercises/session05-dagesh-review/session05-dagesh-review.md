# Session 5 — Dagesh: Forte vs. Lene

**Topic:** BBH Ch3 Review — Identifying Dagesh Forte and Dagesh Lene

---

**Instructions:** For each word, a specific letter with a dagesh is identified in the "Letter" column. Determine whether the dagesh is Forte or Lene, and state the reason.

**Rules to apply:**
- **Dagesh Lene** appears only in the 6 Begadkephat letters (בּ גּ דּ כּ פּ תּ) and only when the letter is not preceded by a vowel in the same word.
- **Dagesh Forte** can appear in any letter except gutturals (א ה ח ע) and Resh (ר). It doubles the letter.
- If the letter is NOT a Begadkephat letter, any dagesh must be **Forte**.
- If the letter IS a Begadkephat letter, look at context: word-initial position after no vowel → usually **Lene**; after a closed syllable or assimilation → **Forte**.

## Answer Key

| # | Word | Target Letter | Dagesh Type | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | הַשָּׁמַיִם | שׁ | **Forte** | שׁ (Shin) is NOT a Begadkephat letter — any dagesh in Shin is Forte. This Forte marks assimilation of the definite article (הַ + שָׁמַיִם → הַשָּׁמַיִם) |
| 2 | בְּרֵאשִׁית | בּ | **Lene** | בּ is a Begadkephat letter at the beginning of a word (after the vocal shewa prefix בְּ / in absolute initial position) — dagesh lene marks the hard pronunciation |
| 3 | כִּי | כּ | **Lene** | כּ is a Begadkephat letter at the beginning of a word — dagesh lene marks the hard k sound |
| 4 | הַמָּיִם | מּ | **Forte** | מּ (Mem) is NOT a Begadkephat letter — any dagesh in Mem is Forte. Assimilation of the definite article (הַ + מַיִם → הַמָּיִם) |
| 5 | תּוֹרָה | תּ | **Lene** | תּ is a Begadkephat letter at the beginning of a word — dagesh lene marks the hard t sound |
| 6 | מִשְׁפָּט | פּ | **Forte** | פּ is a Begadkephat letter, but it follows the closed syllable מִשׁ (silent shewa under Shin closes the syllable). A Begadkephat letter after a closed syllable takes Dagesh Forte, not Lene |
| 7 | גּוֹי | גּ | **Lene** | גּ is a Begadkephat letter at the beginning of a word — dagesh lene marks the hard g sound |
| 8 | וַיִּקְרָא | יּ | **Forte** | יּ (Yod) is NOT a Begadkephat letter — any dagesh in Yod is Forte. The Wayyiqtol prefix וַיִּ- doubles the prefix yod |
| 9 | הַפָּנִים | פּ | **Forte** | פּ is a Begadkephat letter, but it follows the definite article הַ — assimilation of the article causes Dagesh Forte in the following consonant |
| 10 | דְּבַר | דּ | **Lene** | דּ is a Begadkephat letter at word-initial position (the prefix לְ / מְ etc. does not count as a vowel preceding the stem-initial consonant for lene purposes) — dagesh lene marks the hard d |

---

## Key Diagnostic Questions

1. **Is the letter a Begadkephat?** If No → Forte (automatic). If Yes → continue.
2. **Is this word-initial (preceded by no vowel in the same word)?** If Yes → Lene.
3. **Does the preceding syllable close with this consonant?** If Yes → likely Forte.
4. **Is this an article assimilation or nun assimilation?** If Yes → Forte.
