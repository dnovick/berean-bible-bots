## Answer Key

| # | Word | Gloss | Diphthong |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | בַּ֫יִת | house | Pathach + Yod |
| 2 | שָׁמַ֫יִם | heavens | Pathach + Yod |
| 3 | חַי | living, alive | Pathach + Yod |
| 4 | גּוֹי | nation | Holem + Yod |
| 5 | כְּסוּי | a covering | Shureq + Yod |
| 6 | עָלָיו | upon him | Qamets + Yod (before Waw — the Yod quiesces; pronounced as one vowel + Waw) |
| 7 | בָּנָיו | his sons | Qamets + Yod (before Waw — the Yod quiesces; pronounced as one vowel + Waw) |
| 8 | דָּבָר | word | none |
| 9 | מֶ֫לֶךְ | king | none |
| 10 | תּוֹרָה | law, instruction | none (Holem + Waw, but no Yod) |

| 12 | שָׁלוֹם | peace | none |
