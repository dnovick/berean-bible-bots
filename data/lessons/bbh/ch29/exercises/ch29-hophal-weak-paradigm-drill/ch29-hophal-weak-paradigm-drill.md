# Ch29 Hophal Weak Paradigm Drill

**Stem:** Hophal | **Roots:** ילד · קום · עמד · נגד · גלה · שׁלח · מצא · נקם

**Instructions:** For each row, the root and PGN are given. Select (HTML) or write (print) the correct Hophal weak form. Options include forms from Qal, Hiphil, Niphal, Piel, and Hophal — and from other Hophal conjugations of the same root. Identify the correct Hophal form for the PGN shown.

**Note on Imperative and Inf. Construct:** The Hophal Imperative ms is identical in form to the Perfect 3ms for most weak roots — same consonants and vowels; context (command vs. past statement) distinguishes them. Exception: III-ה roots (Imp. ms = הֻגְלֵה with Seghol; Perfect 3ms = הֻגְלָה with Qamets). The Hophal Inf. Construct also equals the Perfect 3ms for most roots. Exception: III-ה roots (Inf. Constr. = הֻגְלוֹת with -וֹת ending).

**Prefix vowel key:** Shureq (הוּ/יוּ/מוּ) — I-י/ו and Biconsonantal roots. Qibbuts (הֻ/יֻ/מֻ) — all other classes. I-guttural: Seghol (הֶ) in the Perfect prefix before ע or א. III-ה: ָה in Perfect 3ms, ֶה in Imperfect; Wayyiqtol is apocopated. I-נ/Geminate: Dagesh forte in R2.
