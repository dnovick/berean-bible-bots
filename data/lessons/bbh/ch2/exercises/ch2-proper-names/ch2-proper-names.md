# Chapter 2 — Sounding Out Proper Names

**Topic:** Hebrew Vowels — Identification in Proper Names

---

**Instructions:** For each proper name, list every vowel sign you see in left-to-right order by its BBH name (e.g., Qamets, Hireq, Shewa).
