Reflection Questions
  1. Qamets vs. Qamets Hatuf (items 1, 3): Both look identical. What two contextual clues help you determine which one you are reading?
  2. Matres lectionis (items 7–8, 10–11): Waw and Yod can serve as vowel letters. For each, list the vowels they signal when quiescent.
  3. Vocal vs. Silent Shewa: The same sign (מְ) marks two different things. What are the rules for determining which type you have?
