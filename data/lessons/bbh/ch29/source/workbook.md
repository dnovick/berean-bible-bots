### III. Grammatical Commentary

**Deuteronomy 10:12**

**יִשְׂרָאֵל** — The proper name יִשְׂרָאֵל is a vocative. The term vocative indicates direct address, explicitly identifying the person to whom the speech is being directed.

**מָה** — The interrogative pronoun מָה is used to ask a question (§8.9). Here it is connected to the Participle שֹׁאֵל: "What does he ask?" or "What is he asking?"

**יְהוָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ** — The divine name and title function as the subject of the Participle שֹׁאֵל.

**מֵעִמָּךְ** — A combination of the preposition מִן, the preposition עִם, and the 2ms pronominal suffix. Translate "from with you" or "of you."

**כִּי אִם** — The combination of כִּי and אִם constitutes a common Hebrew idiom meaning "but, surely, except, unless, only."

**לְיִרְאָה** — The Infinitive Construct forms לְיִרְאָה and לְאַהֲבָה appear with a final Qamets He vowel letter. This phenomenon occurs about one hundred times in the Hebrew Bible, most commonly with the verbal roots ירא (14 times) and אהב (13 times).

**Deuteronomy 10:13**

**לִשְׁמֹר** — The Infinitive Construct beginning verse 13 continues the string of Infinitive Construct forms that began in verse 12.

**מְצַוְּךָ** — Piel Participle ms from צוה with a 2ms pronominal suffix: "commanding you" (§31.4).

**הַיּוֹם** — When the noun יוֹם appears with the definite article, it may be translated "today." When spelled יוֹמָם, it is adverbial and best translated "by day" or "daily."

**לְטוֹב לָךְ** — The preposition לְ with the 2ms pronominal suffix is the ל of possession, "for your good."

**Deuteronomy 10:14**

**הֵן** — The particle הֵן (behold) occurs approximately one hundred times in the Hebrew Bible, mostly in Isaiah (27 times) and Job (32 times). The particle הִנֵּה (behold) is much more common, occurring over a thousand times with the same basic meaning.

**לַיהוָה** — The ל prefixed to the divine name is the ל of possession: "to Yahweh your God belong." There are four objects of possession: הַשָּׁמַיִם (the heavens), שְׁמֵי הַשָּׁמָיִם (the heavens of the heavens), הָאָרֶץ (the earth), and וְכָל־אֲשֶׁר־בָּהּ (and all that is in it).
