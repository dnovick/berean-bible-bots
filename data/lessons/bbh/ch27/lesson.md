*Basics of Biblical Hebrew, Pratico & Van Pelt*

| [I-נ (Pe-Nun)](#6-i-pe-nun-verbs) | נָצַל, נָגַשׁ | Root נ assimilates into R2 with dagesh forte | Imperfect, Wayyiqtol, Imperative, Inf. Construct |

| [Biconsonantal (II-י/ו)](#8-biconsonantal-ii-verbs) | קוּם, שִׂים | Two-consonant root; הֵ in Perfect; qamets prefix in Imperfect | All conjugations |
| [Geminate (II=III)](#9-geminate-ayin-doubled-iiiii-verbs) | סָבַב, שָׁלַל | R2 = R3; forms nearly identical to Biconsonantal | All conjugations |

> **Key principle:** None of these classes requires learning a new stem. Every change follows from a standard phonological rule applied to the strong Hiphil pattern.

## 2. I-Guttural (Pe-Guttural) Verbs

### Pattern

In the Hiphil Perfect, the strong prefix is הִ (hireq). When R1 is a guttural, the prefix vowel shifts to **seghol** (הֶ) and the guttural takes **hateph seghol** (הֶעֱמִיד). In the Imperfect, Wayyiqtol, Imperative, and Inf. Construct, the guttural takes a composite shewa (hateph patach for ע/ח, hateph seghol for א).

**Diagnostic markers:**
- **Perfect 3ms:** הֶעֱמִיד — *seghol* under הֶ; *hateph seghol* under guttural
- **Imperfect 3ms:** יַעֲמִיד — composite shewa under R1; no dagesh in R1
- **Wayyiqtol 3ms:** וַיַּעֲמֵד — composite shewa under R1; *tsere* final
- **Imperative 2ms:** הַעֲמֵד — *patach* under הַ; composite shewa under R1
- **Participle ms:** מַעֲמִיד — *patach* under מַ; composite shewa under R1

## 3. III-ח/ע (Lamed-Guttural) Verbs

### Pattern

When R3 is ח or ע, the gutturals demand an *a*-class vowel before them. The main effect in the Hiphil: **patach furtive** (פַּתַח גְּנוּבָה) appears before final ח/ע in an open, stressed syllable. This is a fleeting glide pronounced *before* the guttural. The 3fp/2fp Imperfect and 2ms/2fp Imperative take **patach** rather than tsere before the guttural.

**Diagnostic markers:**
- **Perfect 3ms:** הִשְׁלִיחַ — *patach furtive* written below, before ח
- **Imperfect 3ms:** יַשְׁלִיחַ — same *patach furtive*
- **Wayyiqtol 3ms:** וַיַּשְׁלַח — *patach* (not *tsere*) before final ח
- **Participle ms:** מַשְׁלִיחַ — *patach furtive* always present before word-final ח/ע

> **Note:** Patach furtive does NOT appear when ח/ע is followed by a suffix (הִשְׁמַעְתִּי — guttural is in a closed syllable).

## 4. III-א (Lamed-Aleph) Verbs

### Pattern

The final א of the root quiesces (becomes silent) in word-final position. The vowel that would stand before it lengthens compensatorily. The Hiphil prefix הִ and characteristic hireq yod are otherwise intact.

**Diagnostic markers:**
- **Perfect 3ms:** הִמְצִיא — *hireq yod* + silent א
- **Imperfect 3ms:** יַמְצִיא — Hiphil *hireq* before silent א
- **Wayyiqtol 3ms:** וַיַּמְצֵא — *tsere* before silent final א
- **Participle ms:** מַמְצִיא — מַ prefix + *hireq* + silent final א

## 5. III-ה (Lamed-He) Verbs

### Pattern

The final ה of these roots is a vowel letter (mater lectionis), not a true consonant. In the Hiphil, III-ה roots show contracted endings throughout. The wayyiqtol **apocopates** in forms without a vocalic sufformative (3ms, 3fs, 2ms, 1cs, 1cp): ה is dropped and the short vowel seghol remains under R3.

**Diagnostic markers:**
- **Perfect 3ms:** הִגְלָה — *qamets* + ה mater
- **Imperfect 3ms:** יַגְלֶה — final *seghol* + ה
- **Wayyiqtol 3ms:** וַיַּגֶל — apocopated; ה dropped; *seghol* under R3
- **Imperative 2ms:** הַגְלֵה — *tsere* + ה
- **Participle ms:** מַגְלֶה — *seghol* + ה

## 6. I-נ (Pe-Nun) Verbs

### Pattern

When R1 = נ, the root נ **assimilates** into R2, producing dagesh forte in R2. This happens in the Imperfect, Wayyiqtol, Imperative, and Infinitive Construct. In the Perfect and Participle, the הִ/מַ prefix stabilizes the root נ so assimilation does NOT occur.

**Diagnostic markers:**
- **Perfect 3ms:** הִצִּיל — *hireq* + *dagesh* in R2 (צ); נ has assimilated
- **Imperfect 3ms:** יַצִּיל — *patach* + *dagesh* in R2
- **Wayyiqtol 3ms:** וַיַּצֵּל — *dagesh* in R2 + *tsere*
- **Imperative 2ms:** הַצֵּל — הַ + *dagesh* in R2 + *tsere*
- **Participle ms:** מַצִּיל — מַ prefix; *dagesh* in R2 + *hireq*

### Full Paradigm

| | Perfect | Imperfect | Imperative | Inf Cstr | Inf Abs | Participle |

## 7. I-י (Pe-Yod) Verbs

### Pattern

Most I-י roots in Biblical Hebrew derive historically from I-ו (Pe-Vav). In the Hiphil, the original ו reappears as **holem waw (וֹ)** following the prefix or preformative, producing הוֹ in the Perfect and יוֹ/תּוֹ in the Imperfect. Apart from the holem waw, all other Hiphil strong verb diagnostics are preserved.

**Diagnostic markers:**
- **Perfect 3ms:** הוֹשִׁיב — הוֹ prefix (*holem waw*) + *hireq* in second syllable
- **Imperfect 3ms:** יוֹשִׁיב — *holem waw* under prefix consonant + *hireq*
- **Wayyiqtol 3ms:** וַיּוֹשֶׁב — *dagesh* in י (contraction of וַיּ) + *holem waw* + *seghol*
- **Imperative 2ms:** הוֹשֵׁב — הוֹ prefix + *tsere*
- **Participle ms:** מוֹשִׁיב — מוֹ prefix (*holem waw*) + *hireq*

## 8. Biconsonantal (II-י/ו) Verbs

### Pattern

Biconsonantal roots have only two true consonants (R1 and R3), with a medial long vowel (historically R2 = י or ו). In the Hiphil, the Perfect prefix is **הֵ** (*tsere*) in third-person forms and **הֲ** (*hateph patach*) in second/first-person forms, which add a holem waw connecting vowel before sufformatives. In the Imperfect, Imperative, and Inf. Construct, the prefix takes **qamets** rather than patach.

**Diagnostic markers:**
- **Perfect 3ms:** הֵקִים — הֵ prefix (*tsere*); *hireq yod* medial
- **Imperfect 3ms:** יָקִים — *qamets* under prefix (not the usual *patach*); *hireq yod*
- **Wayyiqtol 3ms:** וַיָּקֶם — *qamets*; short *seghol* final
- **Imperative 2ms:** הָקֵם — הָ prefix (*qamets*); *tsere* final
- **Participle ms:** מֵקִים — מֵ prefix (*tsere*); *hireq yod*

## 9. Geminate (Ayin-Doubled, II=III) Verbs

### Pattern

Geminate roots (R2 = R3) in the Hiphil produce forms that are nearly **identical** to the Biconsonantal Hiphil. The הֵ prefix in the Perfect, *qamets* under the Imperfect prefix, and מֵ prefix in the Participle are all shared. The only reliable distinguisher is knowing the root: Biconsonantal roots have a hollow vowel letter (ו/י) as medial consonant, while Geminate roots have an identical repeated R2/R3 consonant.

**Diagnostic markers:**
- **Perfect 3ms:** הֵסֵב — *tsere* under הֵ; same as Biconsonantal הֵקִים. Root examination required.
- **Imperfect 3ms:** יָסֵב — *qamets* under prefix consonant; same as Biconsonantal יָקִים
- **Wayyiqtol 3ms:** וַיָּסֶב — *qamets* + contracted root + *seghol*
- **Imperative 2ms:** הָסֵב — *qamets* under הָ
- **Participle ms:** מֵסֵב — *tsere* under מֵ

### Paradigm Summary (3ms per conjugation)

| Conjugation | Geminate (סבב) | Biconsonantal (קום) — for comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Perfect | הֵסֵב | הֵקִים |
| Imperfect | יָסֵב | יָקִים |
| Wayyiqtol | וַיָּסֶב | וַיָּקֶם |
| Weqatal | וְהֵסֵב | וְהֵקִים |
| Imperative | הָסֵב | הָקֵם |
| Inf. Construct | הָסֵב | הָקִים |
| Inf. Absolute | הָסֵב | הָקֵם |
| Participle | מֵסֵב | מֵקִים |

### Key Corpus Examples

- הֵסֵב (1 Kgs 21:4) — "he turned his face toward the wall" — perfect 3ms from סָבַב; הֵ prefix
- וַיָּסֶב (Josh 6:14) — "they marched around the city" — wayyiqtol 3ms; *qamets* + *seghol*
- הָסֵב (2 Sam 2:22) — "turn aside from following me" — imperative 2ms; *qamets* under הָ
- יָסֵב (Isa 44:20) — "a deluded heart has led him astray" — imperfect 3ms; *qamets* prefix
