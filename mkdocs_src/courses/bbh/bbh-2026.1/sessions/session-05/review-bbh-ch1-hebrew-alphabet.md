# Review — BBH Ch1: Hebrew Alphabet

**Lesson:** [BBH Chapter 1 — Hebrew Alphabet](/lessons/hebrew/ch1/)

Key topics to review:
- The 22 consonants and their names (BBH spellings)

- The 6 Begadkephat letters: ב ג ד כ פ ת
- Silent letters and gutturals: א ה ח ע (and ר)
