# Chapter 32 — Pual Strong Morphology Deck

> **Scope note:** This deck provides diagnostic-highlights coverage — not all PGN forms for every conjugation. Weqatal forms are included for 3ms, 2ms, and 1cs for שבר. **Note:** The Pual has no Imperative — passive stems do not take imperatival forms. For full paradigm practice across all PGN slots, use the paradigm drill exercise.

19 cards covering 3 roots across key Pual conjugations for strong verbs, including a compensatory-lengthening root.

**Roots:**
- **שבר** (passive intensive) — to be broken, shattered
- **ספר** (passive simple action) — to be counted, told
- **ברך** (compensatory lengthening, R2=ר) — to be blessed

## Pual — ברך (to be blessed) — Compensatory Lengthening

> **Note:** R2=ר rejects Daghesh Forte throughout. The missing dagesh triggers compensatory lengthening: Qibbuts under R1 lengthens to **Holem** (בֹּ). This Holem is a marker of Pual — contrast the Qal ms Participle (בֹּרֵךְ — rare) and Qal Infinitive Absolute.
