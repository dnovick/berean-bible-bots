Ch35 Hithpael Weak Paradigm Drill

Root: גלה (Part A) / צדק (Part B) / נחם (Part C) / ברך (Part D)  |  Stem: Hithpael

Write the Hithpael Weak forms. Part A uses גלה (III-ה). Part B shows the metathesis rule: when R1 = ז, צ, שׁ, or שׂ, the הִתְ prefix undergoes metathesis (הִצְטַדֵּק). Part C uses נחם (II-Guttural, Virtual Doubling — §35.6-7). Part D uses ברך (II-Guttural, Compensatory Lengthening — §35.8-9).
Metathesis rule: When R1 is ז, צ, שׁ, or שׂ — the ת of הִתְ and R1 switch places: הִתְ + צדק → הִצְטַדֵּק. With שׁ the ת also assimilates: הִשְׁתַּמֵּר.
