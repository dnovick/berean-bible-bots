### III. Grammatical Commentary

**Exodus 34:33**

**וַיְכַל** — The Vocal Shewa under the Imperfect preformative and the Pathach under the first root consonant identifies this verb as Piel. The Daghesh Forte of the Piel stem, expected in the second root consonant, has dropped out due to the absence of the final ֶה (Seghol He) vowel letter in this short form prefixed with Waw Consecutive (17.3.2). The Daghesh Forte of the Waw Consecutive, expected in the preformative consonant, has dropped out due to sqnmlwy (5.5, 30.15).

**וַיִּתֵּן** — HALOT (2:734–35) lists as many as thirteen different meanings for the verbal root נתן in the Qal stem. In this context, "to put, place" makes the most sense.

**Exodus 34:34**

**צֵאתוֹ** — This is the Qal Infinitive Construct from the verbal root יצא with a 3ms pronominal suffix. When it comes to the spelling of the Qal Infinitive Construct, I-י and I-נ verbs may drop the first root consonant and add ת after the verbal root (20.5–6). The 3ms pronominal suffix functions as the subject of the verbal idea, "when he went out."

**וְיָצָא וְדִבֶּר** — These two Perfect 3ms verbs, each prefixed with the Waw Consecutive, continue the modal nuance of יָסִיר (he would remove), "he would go and [he would] tell."

**אֵת אֲשֶׁר** — The definite direct object marker אֵת identifies the following relative clause introduced by אֲשֶׁר as the object of the verb וְדִבֶּר, "and he would tell the people of Israel what he was commanded."

**יְצֻוֶּה** — The diagnostic features of the Pual Imperfect verb are: Vocal Shewa under the Imperfect preformative, Qibbuts under the first root consonant, and Daghesh Forte in the second root consonant. The only difference between the spelling of the Piel and the Pual in this form is the vowel under the first root consonant. In the Pual, the vowel under the first root consonant is Qibbuts. In the Piel, the vowel under the first root consonant is Pathach (e.g., יְצַוֶּה).

**Exodus 34:35**

**וְרָאוּ** — Once again, this Qal Perfect 3cp verb from ראה with Waw Consecutive continues the modal nuance of יָסִיר from verse 34, "and [they] would see."

**עוֹר** — This noun appears in the construct state, "the skin of the face of Moses." Single syllable nouns with an unchangeable long vowel are spelled identically in the absolute and construct states (10.3.3c). The unchangeable long vowel will not reduce in the construct state, though it may be spelled defectively (2.9).

**וְהֵשִׁיב** — The הֵ prefix and the Hireq Yod stem vowel identifies this verb as Hiphil Perfect 3ms from שׁוּב. In the Hiphil stem, Biconsonantal verbs exhibit the diagnostic Hireq Yod stem vowel in most forms regardless of the Biconsonantal verb class (27.14–15).

**אִתּוֹ** — The definite direct object marker and the preposition "with" share identical lexical (אֵת) and construct (אֶת־) forms. However, their spelling is different when taking pronominal suffixes. The definite direct object marker is spelled with an o-class vowel (אֹתוֹ), "him" (except in the 2mp and 2fp). The preposition "with" is spelled with an i-class vowel and Daghesh Forte (אִתּוֹ), "with him" (9.14).
