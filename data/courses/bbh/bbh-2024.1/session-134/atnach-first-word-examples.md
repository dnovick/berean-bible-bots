# Atnach on the First Word — Full List

Companion to [Adventure with Atnach](adventure-with-atnach.md). Every verse in the Hebrew Bible where the atnach (etnahta) falls on the very first word of the verse — found by scanning all 23,217 OT verses in the TAHOT morphology data for a first word carrying the etnahta mark (U+0591).

41 verses total. Two (marked \*) fall in the Aramaic portion of Daniel and are included for completeness, not as Hebrew examples.
