## Answer Key

| # | Word | Gloss | Classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | בֹּרֵחַ | fleeing | Furtive Pathach — under the final ח, pronounced before the guttural |
| 2 | נֹטֵעַ | planting | Furtive Pathach — under the final ע |
| 3 | רָקִיעַ | expanse, firmament | Furtive Pathach — under the final ע |
| 4 | רוּחַ | spirit, wind | Furtive Pathach — under the final ח; the word is monosyllabic |
| 5 | חַטָּאת | sin | Quiescent א — the א has no vowel of its own and is not counted in syllabification (חַטְ׀טָאת) |
| 6 | רֹאשׁ | head | Quiescent א — the א has no vowel of its own, between the Holem and the Shin |
| 7 | מָצָא | he found | neither — the final א carries its own vowel (Qamets), so it is a full consonant, not quiescent |
| 8 | דָּבָר | word | neither |
| 9 | מֶ֫לֶךְ | king | neither |
| 10 | שָׁלוֹם | peace | neither |
