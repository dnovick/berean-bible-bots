# Chapter 28 — Hophal Strong Verb Morphology Deck

*40 cards covering all Hophal conjugations × PGN using strong roots only.*
*Primary paradigm root: **שׁלך** (shin-lamed-kaf, "to cast/throw") — 13 OT Hophal occurrences.*
*Secondary roots where better attested: **פקד** (Perfect 3ms, Lev 6:4), **חרם** (Imperfect 3ms, Lev 27:29), **חרב** (Inf.Abs., 2Ki 3:23).*
*Forms marked "paradigm" have no OT Hophal strong root attestation for that PGN; forms are constructed by regular pattern.*

## Key Diagnostic Summary

| Card Front | Expected Back |
|---|---|
| What vowel marks the Hophal Perfect prefix (strong root)? | **הָ** (qamets hatuf, o-class) OR **הֻ** (qibbuts, u-class) — OT shows both; o-class predominates |
| What vowel marks the Hophal Imperfect prefix? | **יֻ / תֻּ** (qibbuts) — e.g., יֻשְׁלַךְ |
| What vowel marks the Hophal Participle prefix? | **מֻ** (mem + qibbuts) — e.g., מֻשְׁלָךְ |
| Does the Hophal Imperative exist? | Yes — but extremely rare (1 clear OT token: הָפְנוּ Jer 49:8); form identical to Perfect 3ms |
| How to distinguish Hophal Ptc. from Pual Ptc.? | Dagesh Forte in R2 → Pual; no Dagesh in R2 → Hophal |
| Hophal is the passive of which stem? | **Hiphil** (causative active) |
