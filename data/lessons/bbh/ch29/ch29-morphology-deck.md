# Chapter 29 — Hophal Weak Verb Morphology Deck

*44 cards covering all eight Hophal weak root classes.*
*The u-class prefix vowel (Shureq וּ or Qibbuts ֻ) is present in every card — the weak class only changes what happens to the root consonants around it.*

*Primary roots: **קום** (set up) · **מות** (die/put to death) · **שׁוב** (return) · **נגד** (tell/report) · **גלה** (exile) · **ילד** (bear/beget) · **בוא** (bring) · **ירד** (bring down)*

| Card Front | Expected Back |
|---|---|
| What prefix vowel is present in EVERY Hophal form regardless of weak class? | u-class: **Qibbuts (ֻ)** for strong, Pe-Nun, Lamed-weak, Pe-Guttural — **Shureq (וּ)** for Biconsonantal and Pe-Yod/Vav |
| Biconsonantal הוּקַם vs. Pe-Yod הוּלַד — both show Shureq; how to tell apart? | Count root consonants: 2 visible (ק+מ) = Biconsonantal; 3 with first quiesced (ל+ד) = Pe-Yod |
| What does Dagesh forte in R2 signal in a Hophal prefix conjugation? | Pe-Nun assimilation — root נ has assimilated into R2; e.g., יֻגַּד (נגד), יֻכֶּה (נכה) |
| What happened to the ה in וַיֻּגֶל (Lamed-He Wayyiqtol)? | Apocopation — final ה drops in Wayyiqtol; Seghol under last root consonant remains |
| What is מוֹת יוּמַת and why does it have two verb forms? | Legal formula: מוֹת = Qal Inf.Abs. (intensifier) + יוּמַת = Hophal Impf. 3ms; together = "shall SURELY be put to death" |
| Pe-Guttural: what changes when R1 is ע or ח? | Vocal shewa under R1 becomes composite shewa (hateph-patach ֲ for ע/ח); Hophal u-prefix is entirely unaffected |
