Session 5 — Dagesh: Forte vs. Lene

BBH 2026.1 · Ch3 Review · 10 items

Instructions: For each word, a target letter with a dagesh is shown in the "Target" column. Identify whether the dagesh is Forte or Lene, and state the reason.
Diagnostic Rules:
  • Begadkephat letters: בּ גּ דּ כּ פּ תּ — only these can take Dagesh Lene.
  • If the target letter is not a Begadkephat → Forte (automatic).
  • If the target letter is a Begadkephat at word-initial position → Lene.
  • If the target letter follows a closed syllable or an assimilated letter → Forte.
