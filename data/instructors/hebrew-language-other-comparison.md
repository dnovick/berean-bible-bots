Scope: resources tagged with a Hebrew-language subject (e.g. `Hebrew language`, `Hebrew language, Biblical`, `Hebrew poetry, Biblical`) that are **not** already listed in `hebrew-grammar-comparison.md` (grammar textbooks/workbooks/courseware) or `hebrew-texts-comparison.md` (primary Hebrew Bible/manuscript text editions).
